About

I study systems.
I try to change them.

PhD researcher at UCL, startup builder, writer, and co-host of 2Humans. Based in London.

In brief

Research, writing, and building are one practice.

I work at the intersection of energy, equity, and institutional design. Research helps me understand the system; writing makes the questions clearer; building tests whether an idea survives contact with reality.

I am especially interested in people working across disciplines, and in teams ambitious enough to improve how essential systems serve society.
